GeoAttend Privacy Policy

GeoAttend by Optisware · Last updated 16 October 2026

Who we are

GeoAttend is a staff attendance system provided by Optisware. Institutions (such as schools and businesses) use GeoAttend to record when their staff check in and out. This policy explains what personal data the GeoAttend mobile app and admin dashboard collect, why, and how it is handled.

The institution you work for decides to use GeoAttend and manages your account. For questions about how your institution uses your attendance data, contact your institution's administrator. For questions about this policy or GeoAttend itself, contact Optisware at optisware@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, Staff ID, email address, phone number, department and position, and your institution.
  • Sign-in information: a securely hashed password if you sign in with a password. If you choose Continue with Google, we receive your Google account's name, email address, profile photo and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Location: while the GeoAttend app is open, it uses your device's location to show your distance from your workplace. We record your location when you check in or out, and when the open app detects you have left your institution's geofence, so attendance can be verified. GeoAttend does not track your location in the background when the app is closed.
  • Attendance and leave records: check-in and check-out times, method (GPS or QR code), lateness, overtime, and leave requests you submit.
  • Device information: device identifier, model, operating system version, IP address, and a push notification token used to send you attendance reminders.
  • Camera: used only to scan attendance QR codes. Images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Biometrics: if you turn on fingerprint or Face ID sign-in, the check happens entirely on your device. GeoAttend never receives your biometric data.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To record and verify attendance, including checking that you are within your institution's geofence.
  • To let your institution's administrators view attendance, approve leave and produce reports.
  • To send attendance reminders and notifications.
  • To detect misuse, such as attendance fraud, and to keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Your institution: administrators of the institution you belong to can see your account details, phone number, attendance records and leave requests.
  • Optisware: our team can access data when needed to operate, support and secure the service.
  • Service providers: we use trusted providers to run GeoAttend, including cloud hosting and database providers, Google (for Google sign-in), and Expo (for push notifications). They process data only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law.

Google user data

When you use Google sign-in, GeoAttend requests only your basic profile (name and profile photo) and email address. We use this information solely to create or sign in to your GeoAttend account and to show your name to your institution. We do not share Google user data with third parties except as described in this policy, and we do not use it for advertising. GeoAttend's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your institution uses GeoAttend and your account exists. When an administrator deletes your account, or when an institution is removed from GeoAttend, the associated account, attendance and leave records are deleted.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and access to institution data is restricted by role. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view your profile information in the GeoAttend app.
  • You can deny location or camera permission in your device settings, but attendance check-in may then not work.
  • To correct or delete your information, contact your institution's administrator, or email optisware@gmail.com.
  • You can remove GeoAttend's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

GeoAttend is intended for staff and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
